📍 About Propel Labs

The Propel Labs are designed to provide students with the resources needed to turn theoretical ideas into working prototypes. These labs are equipped with 3D printers, laser cutters, and advanced electronics testing equipment.

🌟 Key Objectives

  • » To provide a platform for multi-disciplinary project work.
  • » To encourage students to participate in national and international competitions.
  • » To support startup incubation and IPR filing.
