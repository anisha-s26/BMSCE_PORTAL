The BMSCE Placement Roadmap

1. Student Registration

Students enroll with the Placement Cell, upload verified CVs, and select preferred tracks.

2. Training & Skill-Up

Comprehensive training covering quantitative aptitude, soft skills, and technical mock interviews.

3. Eligibility Check

Review of academic records (CGPA 6.0+) and backlog status against company criteria.

4. Drive Scheduling

Coordination of campus visits or virtual drives with detailed job descriptions shared in advance.

5. Recruitment Rounds

Final selection involving online assessments, GD, Technical, and HR rounds.

Key Placement Regulations

Students with confirmed offers will not be allowed to participate in further drives without prior approval.

Professional conduct and discipline are mandatory throughout all placement activities.
