Department of Computer Science & Engineering

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering at BMSCE was established to produce graduates with strong fundamentals in the CSE domain. The department offers a robust BE program with a focus on cutting-edge research and technological innovations to address the challenges of the digital age.

Faculty Achievements

Vision

To be a pioneer and internationally recognized institute in Computer Science and Engineering, leading the way in cutting-edge research, technological innovations, and producing highly skilled professionals to address the challenges and opportunities of the digital age.

Mission

  • Providing comprehensive and cutting-edge education in Computer Science and Engineering, equipping students with the knowledge and skills needed to succeed in the rapidly evolving tech industry.
  • Fostering collaborative and interdisciplinary culture within the department, encouraging students and faculty to explore innovative solutions through cross-disciplinary projects and partnerships.

Outcomes & Objectives

Program Educational Objectives (PEOs):

  • PEO1: Develop technical proficiency to solve a wide range of problems in industry, government and other work environments.
  • PEO2: Adopt quickly to new technologies and environments to progress in research, innovation and entrepreneurship.
  • PEO3: Possess the ability to collaborate as team members and team leaders to facilitate cutting-edge technical solutions.
