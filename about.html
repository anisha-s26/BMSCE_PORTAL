About the Institution

The B.M.S. College of Engineering (BMSCE) was founded in the year 1946 by Sri. B. M. Sreenivasaiah.

It is the first private sector initiative in engineering education in India. Over the past 77 years, the institution has produced more than 40,000 engineers contributing globally.

Vision

Promoting prosperity of mankind by augmenting human resource capital through quality education.

Mission

Accomplish excellence in Technical Education through Research and Service to society.
